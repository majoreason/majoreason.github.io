Major Eason

Hello! My name is Major Eason and I am an incomng fourth year PhD student in Sociology at Harvard University.

My mixed-method research agenda is broadly built around investigating the intersection of environmental racism and the political economy of place. I examine this through a) Quantitative analyses analyzing the distribution of toxic industrial facilities and their economic impacts on communities and b) A qualitative case study of how community leaders in a predominantly Black community, or racial middlemen, negotiate their current political economic state. This research agenda both emerges and looks to inform the pressing need to develop economically and environmentally sustainable forms of localized development in marginalized communities.
